About Us

Company History

Founded in 2016, SPACES set out to bring premium veneers, plywoods, laminates, and decorative panels to the forefront of interior design. From a modest beginning, we've grown into a trusted brand among architects, designers, and contractors. With a focus on quality, innovation, and customer service, SPACES continues to help transform spaces into timeless, functional environments built on craftsmanship and trust.

Our Mission

At SPACES, our mission is to deliver high-quality veneers, plywoods, laminates, and decorative panels that combine design, durability, and sustainability. We aim to inspire creativity, support professionals, and enhance every space through innovative materials, reliable service, and a deep commitment to craftsmanship and customer satisfaction.
